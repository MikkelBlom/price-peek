# Price Peek privacy policy

Last updated: 19 October 2026

Price Peek does not collect, store, sell or share any personal data.

**What it reads.** To find prices, Price Peek reads the text of the web pages you visit, including numbers you type into fields. This happens only inside your browser. Page content is never sent anywhere or saved.

**What it stores.** Your settings (target currency, on/off switches, which currencies to detect) and the latest exchange rates are saved in your browser's local extension storage. They never leave your device.

**Network requests.** Once a day or less, Price Peek downloads exchange rates from api.frankfurter.dev, with open.er-api.com as a fallback. These requests contain no information about you or the pages you visit. Like any web request, they reveal your IP address to those services; their own privacy policies apply.

**No tracking.** No analytics, no ads, no accounts, no cookies, no third-party scripts.

**Contact.** Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/MikkelBlom/price-peek/issues.
