## Title

Price Peek: any price, in your currency (Chrome extension)

## Description

Price Peek is a free Chrome extension that finds prices on any website and shows what they cost in your own currency, as a small badge right on the price.

• 11 currencies: Korean won, US dollar, euro, British pound, Japanese yen, Chinese yuan, Swiss franc, Thai baht, and Danish, Swedish and Norwegian kroner
• Reads local formats like 3억 5,000만원, 3万円 and 19,99 €
• Pick your target currency in one click
• Works on numbers you type, too: price filters, budgets
• Never changes the page; daily exchange rates from the European Central Bank
